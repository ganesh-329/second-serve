## 1. The decisions, up front

1. **22 chapters (ch 1 to 22) replace the old 15.** Old Arc 1 is 47,500 words in 15 chapters (2,084 to 4,559 each). At Arc 3's density, with the reported canon scenes staged and the rallies that matter told serve, pass, set, hit, where, why, the same material runs about 120,000 to 130,000 words. That is 22 chapters of 5,000 to 6,500. Nothing is padded: every added scene is a canon conversation that the old draft summarized, a protected moment it skipped (Ukai's yes, Daichi's Flightless Crows speech, Kageyama's "I will not lose", Suga asking Ukai to pick Kageyama, the eyes-open quick through Inuoka, Ukai's "grand stage"), or a stretch of a match the old draft did in 1 line.
2. **Splits.** The 3v3 day becomes 3 chapters (5, 6, 7: set 1 to 20-16; 20-16 to set 2's start; set 2 and the evening). The days before it get their own chapter (4). Seijoh becomes 3 (8 the lineup and the bus; 9 sets 1 and 2; 10 set 3, the gate, the bus home). The Asahi week becomes 4 (12 Thursday and Friday; 13 the Takeda POV weekend; 14 Ukai's 1st practice; then the match). The Neighborhood match becomes 2 (15, 16). Golden Week becomes 3 (17, 18, 19 the Daichi POV). Nekoma becomes 3 (20 set 1; 21 set 2; 22 the rematches and home).
3. **2 surprise POV chapters: ch 13 Takeda, ch 19 Daichi.** 6 chapters apart, each covers new time, each owns a protected moment Sho can't be inside (Ukai agreeing to coach; Suga asking Ukai to pick Kageyama, and the captain's "never beat Nekoma"). Section 4 says why these 2 and why not Suga, Mio, Kenma, Tanaka or Ukai.
4. **The year shape is 2013's** (Mon Apr 8 is day 1; Thu May 2 to Mon May 6 is Golden Week camp, the match on Mon May 6, a national holiday that year). Arc 4's plan already uses 2013's shape for August to October of the same school year (S4:30), so Arc 1 must match it. Canon's year is 2012 [wiki: Sh_y_Hinata, heights dated Apr 2012]; only weekdays change, and the fic names no weekday that breaks.
5. **The eye fires exactly 4 times** (ch 6 twice, ch 20 once, ch 21 once), as the old text does and a2:4761 counts. No firing moves; 2 rule fixes (it shows the arm, never the landing; it is never announced aloud).
6. **The 3 status cards stay as written** and sit at the end of new ch 1, ch 6 and ch 18.
7. **Canon restored where the old draft broke a protected moment or a voice:** Hinata's ace vow in the wig scene; Daichi's Flightless Crows speech (ch 2); Kageyama's "I will not lose" to Suga (ch 7); Kageyama's "we" to Kindaichi (ch 10); Daichi's shoulder slap to Asahi (ch 13); Ukai's yes "only until the Nekoma match" on the page (ch 13); Hinata's "strongest decoy" and Asahi's "the coolest is the one they fear" (ch 16); Suga asking Ukai to pick Kageyama (ch 19); the 7-5 eyes-open whiff, the float toss, the Small Giant story told to Takeda, Hinata finding the path himself and hitting it out (ch 21); Nekoma leading 18-15 (ch 21); the last rally's eyes-open quick through Inuoka, Kai's save and Kenma's ball over (ch 21); Ukai's "on that grand stage" and the post-match pairs (ch 22).
8. **Where reports disagree, decided here** (reasons in each chapter and in section 7): Iwaizumi's "There's 2 of them now" is cut, the look stays (ch 10); Suga says "Ennoshita" (ch 8); Yamaguchi asks Shimada himself, after Sho brings him over, so "I'm the one who introduced you" (a2:285) stays true (ch 16); "Observation Haki" stays in Sho's head (ch 6); Nekoma's set 2 runs canon's way (Nekoma 18-15) with Fukunaga in the back row (ch 21); the extra games are canon's 2 more, so 3 games and 6 sets in all (ch 22, flagged); "past life" is said 0 times in Arc 1 (Suga's ch 16 use in Arc 2 is the 1 in the book); Sho stays out of Takeda's persuasion (R9 over 06's joined bow); "Allergies." stays as Sho's 1 dodge (06 over 04 and 07).
