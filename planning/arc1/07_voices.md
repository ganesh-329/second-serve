## 0. Headline findings

1. **Arc 1 and the first 2 chapters of Arc 2 are a different book from ch 18 onward.** From ch 18 ("Dialogue written under the Oct 30 rules", the ch 18 continuity block says so itself [a2:1582]) the conversations are already in canon voice and already in Arc 3's style: Kageyama's bottle talk [a2:1446-1504], Ukai's read-blocking lesson [a2:1352-1380], Suga's "I don't *know* yet!" [a2:2836], the notebook to Ukai [a2:2917-2979], the drainpipe [a2:5004-5066], the whole Mio POV. Ch 1 to 17 are the problem. I audited 238 conversations: 150 KEEP, 66 TRIM, 22 REBUILD. Arc 1 (143): 73 KEEP, 54 TRIM, 16 REBUILD. Ch 16 to 17 (22): 6 KEEP, 10 TRIM, 6 REBUILD. Ch 18 to 29 (73): 71 KEEP, 2 TRIM (both name slips), 0 REBUILD. Every REBUILD is in ch 1 to 17.
2. **The single biggest fault is that canon characters exist to set up or receive Sho's punchline.** 46 of the 165 conversations in ch 1 to 17 have a canon person as a straight man (most often Tsukishima, Tanaka, Ukai, Kuroo, Suga and Daichi, in that order). Canon Tsukishima wins exchanges; canon Kuroo needles and wins; canon Ukai doesn't banter with a 1st year, he gives orders and explains. In the fic they keep asking the question that lets Sho land a line.
3. **Sho's quips: about 81 in Arc 1 and 30 in ch 16 to 17, close to 0 in ch 18 to 29.** The shamelessness has turned into wit. Lloyd Frontera's shamelessness is asking for things and not minding the no. In Arc 3 it reads right: "Please bully me too. The same as Lev. I'll be quieter than him." [a3:2462]; "I've been away for a week. I've lost a lot of weight. For the team." "It's 120, Kiyose." [a3:5558-5560]. Both are plain asks that lose.
4. **Earnest canon moments are summarized while Sho's quips are quoted.** 12 conversations that carry canon weight (Suga's envy, Tanaka's pep talk, Noya on Asahi, Suga's confession, Kageyama's decoy talk, Oikawa at the gate) are reported speech, so the reader hears Sho's jokes and never hears the canon voices that matter.
5. **Voice slips with a canon source:** Shimada calls Yamaguchi "Tadashi" in canon [wiki: Makoto_Shimada], the fic uses "Yamaguchi" [a2:4245]; Oikawa calls Hinata "Chibi-chan" in canon (the wiki's alias list for Hinata), the fic has "Shrimpy-chan" [a2:738]; canon Yamaguchi walks up to Shimada and asks himself [wiki: Preparing_for_Flight], the fic has Sho ask for him [a1:4299]; Suga calls Ennoshita "Chikara" [a1:2054]. Full list in section 4.
6. **The fix is already written.** Arc 2 ch 18 onward and Arc 3 are the template. The rebuilt samples in section 3 copy their moves: people answer sideways, the sharp line belongs to the sharp character, a want sits under every line, care goes through an object, and the scene ends on an action.

## 1. Canon voice bible

Format for each person: **Address** (what they call others, what others call them). **Sentences** (length, habits, verbal tics). **Never.** **Reacts to** praise / being asked a favor / a stranger / bad news. **Canon lines** (3 to 5, quoted from the wiki dump; where the dump has no direct quote I say so and use reported speech from a chapter page, marked "reported"). **In the fic** (what's off in Arc 1 to 2, what already works, with Arc 3 lines that hit the target).

Honorific rule of thumb for the whole team: 1st years to 2nd and 3rd years use -san (Kageyama, Tsukishima: Daichi-san, Sugawara-san, Tanaka-san) or -senpai (Hinata and Sho to Tanaka and Noya, because those 2 melt at it [wiki: Ry_nosuke_Tanaka; Y_Nishinoya]); 3rd years and 2nd years use bare surnames down; same-year boys use bare surnames ("Kageyama", "Hinata", "Tsukishima"). Sho is the exception the fic has chosen: he's polite to classmates too ("Kageyama-kun" from ch 15 [a1:5506] and all through Arc 2). Keep that, but use it from ch 3, so it reads as his habit. Kageyama and Tsukishima never return the -kun.

### 4.1 The checklist (run it on every conversation before it goes in)

1. **Wants.** Write each speaker's want in 3 to 6 words above the scene. A speaker with no want is a prop: give them one or cut them.
2. **Who's allowed to be dry?** Tsukishima, Suga (gently), Oikawa, Kuroo, Ukai (gruffly), Kenma (3 words), Dad (1 line a chapter). If Sho or anyone else has the sharpest line in the scene, rewrite it.
3. **Last word.** If Sho has the last line of the conversation, check it. More than once a chapter, give it to the other person or end on an action.
4. **Mirror test.** Read every reply against the line before it. If it reuses the other person's words to top them, cut or rewrite it as a sideways answer.
5. **Setup test.** For every question a canon person asks: is it what they want to know, or only a way to let Sho answer? If the 2nd, rewrite the question.
6. **Word list.** Teenagers never say aloud: investment, data, sample (size), consistency, strategy, contract, terms, inflation, interest, retroactive, peer review, manifesto, budget, stat, rate, protocol, optimize, efficiency, analysis. Sho can think them, rarely.
7. **Numbers.** Is any number a joke? Is anyone keeping a ledger (buns, puddings, wipes, debts, sins)? 1 count a chapter at most, and only if a real person would count it.
8. **Address.** Check every name against section 1: Chibi-chan, Tobio-chan, Iwa-chan, Tadashi, Ennoshita, Kiyose, -san up, -senpai from Hinata and Sho to Tanaka and Noya, -kun from Takeda.
9. **Shameless test.** Is Sho's ask plain ("Please." "Could I...?" "Teach me.")? Does he get more polite as he wants it more? Does he take the no, at most 1 repeat?
10. **Canon move.** Does each canon person do their own canon thing in the scene (section 1, "Sentences")? Kageyama literal, Tsukishima's offer-dig, Tanaka's face or tears, Noya's declaration, Daichi's smile, Suga's touch and name, Asahi's apology, Ennoshita's 1 read, Kiyoko's 1 line, Ukai's proper explanation, Takeda's bow and long sentence, Oikawa's cheer then knife, Kuroo's poke, Kenma's 3 words.
11. **Ownership.** Whose moment is this in canon? If it's someone else's, Sho steps to the edge and holds something.
12. **Heard, not told.** If a canon line carries weight, does the reader hear it in the canon person's voice? Summary is fine for overheard scraps; the line that matters is quoted.
13. **Objects.** Is the care in the scene carried by a thing (milk, a bottle with the cap loosened, edamame, a fan, a towel), with nobody naming it?
14. **Ending.** Read the last 2 lines aloud. If they're a setup and a punchline, cut the punchline and end on what someone does.
15. **Arc 3 test.** Would this exchange sit in ch 41 to 45 next to "If it comes to me, I'm finishing it." "Okay."? If it would stick out, rewrite it.
16. **Mio test.** Is she 10: blunt, illogical, demanding, scared underneath? No contracts, no economics, no stand-up.
17. **Mouth test.** Would a 15-year-old say this at 4:30 a.m. with rice in his mouth? If only a writer would, cut it.
