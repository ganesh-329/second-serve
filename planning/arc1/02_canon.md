## 7. Volleyball rules (memory; Japanese high school)
- Rally scoring: sets to 25 (win by 2), deciding set to 15 (win by 2) [memory].
- Rotation: 6 players, clockwise rotation upon winning serve; positions: front row 4/3/2 (left/middle/right), back row 5/6/1; server is position 1. Overlap: at the moment of serve, each player must be positioned relative to adjacent neighbors in rotation order (front row vs back row, left vs right) [memory].
- Libero: wears a different color, replaces back-row players only, may not attack above the net, may not serve in international (older) rules and in Japanese HS rules I recall libero does not serve [unsure on current JVA HS rules; the fic should avoid showing the libero serving]; no counted substitution for libero replacement. Libero may not set overhand from in front of the 3m line for an attack [memory].
- Substitutions: up to 6 per set under standard FIVB; Japanese high school rules also 6 per set, with a player re-entering only to their original position once [memory; [unsure] on JVA specifics].
- Timeouts: 2 per set, 30 sec each; technical timeouts exist in some tournaments [memory].
- Practice matches: formats are by agreement; Karasuno vs Seijoh was 3 sets (2:1) [web]; Nekoma match 3 sets [web].
