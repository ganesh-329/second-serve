## 0. Rules this report stands on

Indoor 6-a-side (FIVB). Article numbers from memory; the bible already cites the 2025 to 2028 rules modification PDF ([web: https://vbahk.org.hk/items/media/Refereeing/202528_Volleyball_Rules/VB/fivbvolleyball_rulesmodification20252028.pdf]) and the FIVB rules landing page exists ([web: https://www.fivb.com/volleyball/the-game/official-volleyball-rules/]).

| Rule | What it means on the page | Tag |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rotation (7.6) | The team that wins a rally while RECEIVING rotates 1 place clockwise, and the player moving from position 2 to position 1 serves. A team that wins while SERVING keeps the same server. So a team never serves after losing a rally. | [memory] |
| Serving order | Positions 1 to 6 hold the serving order. The 3 front-row players are always 3 players in a row in that order, so a setter and the player 3 places after him (the "opposite") are never front row together, and in a standard order (S, OH1, MB1, OPP, OH2, MB2) the 2 middles are never front row together and neither are the 2 outsides. | [memory] |
| Positional fault at the serve | Only at the moment of the serve: each front-row player must be in front of his back-row partner, and left/right order must hold (4 left of 3 left of 2). After contact anyone goes anywhere. So "position 2 at the left edge of the receive line" is a fault. | [memory] |
| Back-row attack (13.2.2) | A back-row player can attack a ball above the net only if he takes off from behind the 3 m line. | [memory] |
| Back-row block (14.6.2) | A back-row player can't complete a block or be part of one. A back-row player touching a blocked ball at the net is a fault. | [memory] |
| Held ball (9.2.2) | The ball must be hit, never caught or held. A setter who "holds the ball" a beat in his hands commits a fault. A setter can delay by waiting with hands up and letting the ball drop lower before contact. | [memory] |
| Substitution (15.5, 15.6) | 6 per team per set. A starter can leave and come back once per set, only in his own place in the order. A sub can come in once per set, only for the starter he replaced, and only that starter can replace him. | [memory] |
| Libero (19) | Back row only; can't serve (FIVB), can't attack above net height, can't complete a block; replacements are free and unlimited, back-row player only. An overhand set by the libero in the front zone can't be attacked above the net. The fic's dad line "the libero can't serve" (a1:2761) is right under FIVB. | [memory]; bible B:186 cites 19.4.2.1 for re-designation |
| Serve that hits a teammate | Fault: the ball has to cross the net. Point to the receiving team. | [memory] |
| Block-out | Ball off the block and out = attacker's point (Sho explains it right, a1:2731). | [memory] |
| Jump serve landing | Take off behind the end line, land anywhere. Oikawa landing a meter inside is legal (a1:2489). | [memory] |
| 3v3 | No official rules. Canon's 3v3 uses a full court and normal scoring [memory, unsure]. House rules this report uses: 25-point sets, best of 3, rally scoring, servers rotate in a fixed order on every side-out, no positional faults, no back-row rules (everybody may block and attack). | [unsure] |
