## 1. The arc's central question and answer

**Surface question (the bible's):** Can he play all 3 sets?

**Real question:** Can Sho use the eye as a weapon without turning into Satoshi with the knee? Said in the arc's own gesture: **will his hand go up?**

The hand carries 3 meanings in this story and the narration never spells them out:
1. Satoshi's hand that stayed down in Osaka (didn't tell anyone, played through it).
2. Asahi's hand that learned to go up and ask for the ball ("SUGA! ONE MORE!").
3. Sho's hand, flat at shoulder height, asking to come off.

**Answer (in 3 steps, each costing more):**
1. **Yes, and it costs the team** (camp 1, vs Nekoma, at set point). He keeps the rule blindly.
2. **Almost not** (camp 2, vs Fukurodani: 2 rallies late). He confesses 1st and pays with a bench day and a lost night in the 3rd gym.
3. **He never has to**, because he lets go of the eye in time (camp 2, last day, vs Nekoma). He plays all 3 sets, loses, and does the penalty dive laughing.

**The weapon lesson the arc earns (soul version of the power plan):** the eye's 1st read is the weapon; **holding it is the knee.** In ch 22 he held it for 8 rallies. In Arc 3 he learns, on the page and by data, to take the 1st read and let go, and to build a map light enough that the eye costs less when it comes. "Build the map so I don't need the eye" turns out to have a 2nd half nobody told him: a lighter map also makes the eye cheaper.

**What stays unanswered (the Arc 4 hook):** he only proved it at a camp where nothing counts. Mio's ch 26 sentence still stands: "If it's the last set of something... I don't know what I'll do." Spring High's Miyagi final against Shiratorizawa goes 5 sets [memory: canon final is 5 sets]. The last line of Arc 3 should be the new notebook's 1st page with *Play all 3 sets* replaced by *Play all 5 sets.*

**How Arc 3 changes him (checklist for the outline):**
- Hides the cost -> declares the cost (Coach 1st, Mom same day), and tells exactly 1 person all of it (Mio).
- "Every minute on the bench is the monitor room" -> the room with monitors is something he can be offered and politely refuse; a bench in the light, with people who know, is a different place.
- Holds the eye -> takes the 1st read and lets go.
- Waits for the eye -> builds feet that don't wait (Ikkei).
- Satoshi's grief buried -> spoken once, as a stranger's name (Matsumoto, number 5), to Hinata.
- Does NOT change: shameless, cold, counting, never scared of his own power (it's data), still wants the court more than anything, still can't promise what he'll do at the last set of something real.

### Beat 1. The doorway (camp 1, morning of day 1; Sho POV; about ch 32)

**Scene.** The 1st morning in the Tokyo-area gym. 4 teams warming up at once. Sho reaches the door with his bag and stops.

**What he gets hit by.** Everything Satoshi lost, all at once and from the playing side: 8 balls hitting the floor at different rhythms, floor wax, a coach's whistle, a big voice yelling for "one more" (Bokuto), a setter on the far court in a number 5 jersey (Akaashi wears 5; Satoshi wore 5 in college; Sho clocks the number and says nothing). His left hand goes to his left knee, a beat too long (ch 12 did this once; this is its 2nd and last use before the barbecue).

**Who wants what.** Tanaka wants to beat Tokyo and doesn't want a 1st year blocking the door ("Oi. Move. You're in the door."). Hinata wants all of it at once and is already through the door. Sho wants this so much that it stops him for 1 beat (the bible's rattle rule), then he analyzes (4 courts, 6 teams, he counts serve types before he's put his bag down) and goes in.

**The grief, carried by an action: the penalty dives.** Karasuno loses a lot (canon), and every loss means flying-fall laps. Everybody hates them. Sho laughs on the floor. Not performatively: a short helpless laugh with his cheek on the wood. Satoshi spent his last college summer on crutches. Being *punished for losing a match you played in* is a luxury he never got back.
- Tsukishima, landing next to him: "You're enjoying this." / "Yes." / Tsukishima looks at him a little longer than he looks at most things (the ch 22 wording), and dives.
- Do NOT explain the laugh in narration. One Satoshi image at most, and it should be small (a crutch tip squeaking on a gym floor).

**Hook out.** Tomorrow is Nekoma. Kenma, from the other court, watched #13 laugh during a penalty and didn't understand it.

### Beat 2. The hand goes up, and it costs the team (camp 1, vs Nekoma; KENMA POV; about ch 33)

**Scene.** Practice match vs Nekoma. Lev is new and raw (canon), Hinata is trying to keep his eyes open on the quick and it keeps failing (canon). Ukai brings Sho in for receive or for a Tanaka/Asahi rotation. Karasuno reaches **set point**. Karasuno has never beaten Nekoma (ch 13: "The Little Giant's team never beat Nekoma." "So beat them.").

**What happens.** Late in the set, the eye fires on its own (#5) on a Nekoma hitter (Yamamoto's cross or Lev's wild swing). Sho digs a ball nobody should dig, the rally goes on, and the burning comes with it, at once, dry and hot. In the middle of the rally his right hand goes up flat to shoulder height. The rally ends. Ukai subs him off with no word, exactly as promised. Nekoma serves into the reshuffled receive, Kenma runs his offense free, Karasuno loses the set and the match. Penalty dives.

**Why Kenma's eyes.** This is the beat where the weapon gets *read*. Kenma, a gamer who treats a match like a game, sees what Oikawa saw and puts a name on it in his own vocabulary: #13 has a skill with a **cooldown**. Every so often he is early, impossibly. Right after, for a while, he is ordinary and a little slow, and he squints. Kenma plans to send the next ball into the cooldown. Then #13 puts his hand up and walks off before Kenma can use it. Kenma's reaction is dry and exact: he logged out. Smart. Annoying.
- Kenma wants: to finish with minimum effort; to keep watching Shoyo (canon interest, his); to not be the one being read. #13 reads him, which he dislikes.
- Kuroo wants: to provoke and to know. "What'd you do to notebook?" / Kenma: "Nothing. He quit before I could." (The word "quit" matters: it's Mio's word. Kenma uses it without knowing.)
- Lev wants: to be the ace by tomorrow. 1 line to Sho at most ("You're a 1st year? You play like an old man.") Use once in the arc.
- Ukai wants: his rule to mean something. He keeps it: no question, no look, even at set point ("*Especially* then").

**Sho from outside, the reader's new view:** a tall 1st year raising his hand like a student in class, in the middle of a rally, at set point, and walking off. From the other side of the net it looks either cowardly or very strange. Kenma decides it's neither.

**Cost.** The team's 1st real chance at Nekoma. On the scoreboard. Readers should feel the set slip.

**Hook out (Kenma's last thought):** #13 will come back with the cooldown patched, or he won't come back at all. Kenma would rather he patched it. That's more interesting.

### Beat 4. Five puddings (between camps, at home; Sho POV; about ch 35). THE BROKEN PROMISE

**Scene.** Genkan, evening. Mio does her door check (knees, then arms). He tells her: it came, it burned, he put his hand up, how long, where it hurt. She keeps the terms exactly: "What was it like?"

**What he does.** He gives her the cover story everyone else has: "Like looking too hard." She watches his mouth (ch 23: "He does his mouth like that when he says it's fine and it's not"). "That's not all of it."

**Why he can't (the real reason, in his head, 2 lines max):** telling her what it's like means telling her he can *hold* it. Holding it means Seijoh was on purpose. By her ch 23 rule, on purpose counts as quitting, and she wins. Worse: she'd know he chose to bleed. He is protecting her from the knowledge that he chose it, and he's calling that protecting her.

**The payment.** He walks to the konbini by the shrine and buys 5 puddings and lines them up in the fridge, on her shelf.

**What Mio does with them: nothing.** She doesn't eat them. They sit there through the whole interlude: through the Hinata/Kageyama fight, through Ikkei, through the morning they leave for camp 2. Each time Sho opens the fridge, 5 puddings in a row. Mom notices and says nothing (she notices everything). Dad moves them to fit the milk in and puts them back in the same order. This is the cost: Mio didn't want puddings. She wanted to not be scared.

- Mio wants: to know, so she can stop checking his knees at the door.
- Sho wants: to keep the bet alive and her unafraid, and he's wrong about how.
- The morning of camp 2, Mio is not at the door. The 1st time since April. (Hook.)

**Rule for writing it:** no fight scene. Mio's whole reaction is the uneaten puddings and the empty doorway. 1 exchange, then objects.

**Why this is "the eye as a weapon, as a CHOICE":** for the 1st time he uses it on purpose. **Hook out (Akaashi's last thought, echoing Sho's own unwritten Kunimi thought from ch 20/24):** *He's saving something. I'd like to know what for.*

**Next Sho chapter opens with "Tell Coach 1st":** 6:30 a.m., Ukai with his coffee. "Last night it came. In the 3rd gym. I held it 1 ball. On purpose. It burned. I put my hand up." "On purpose." ### Beat 10. All 3 sets: the release (camp 2, last day, vs Nekoma; Sho POV; about ch 41). THE DEFINING CHOICE

**The choice.** He lets go. On purpose. Like letting out a breath he'd been holding (find a better plain image in the drafting; no anime reference here, this one is his). He chooses the next 15 minutes on the court over the next point.

## 5. Mush risks and how to avoid them

1. **Confession fatigue.** 4 promises x 4 firings could become 16 "I told X" scenes. Fix: each promise is dramatized ONCE in full (hand: B2; Coach 1st: B6/B7; Mom: B3; Mio: B4 and B12). Every other time it's a line, a number, or offscreen ("He called Mom. 6.").
2. **Everybody supportive.** Needs friction: Hinata yells; Ukai punishes; Mio refuses the puddings; Kenma exploits; Lev and Kuroo mock the hand ("Notebook raises his hand in class."); Mom sides against him in 1 word; Nekomata's praise is a trap.
3. **Satoshi flashbacks.** Max 1 Satoshi image per chapter, 1 to 3 lines, concrete (crutch tip, tape, the hospital tray already used). The name is said aloud once (B12).
4. **Sho crying.** He cried at the dinner in ch 26. No tears from Sho in Arc 3. His grief comes out as the laugh on the floor and the hand on the knee. Others may cry (Tanaka always can).
5. **The hand as a symbol.** The narrator never connects Osaka's hand, Asahi's hand and Sho's hand. Asahi says his line about asking for the ball; Sho doesn't reply to the subtext; the reader does the work.
6. **The Hinata mirror.** Never stated by anyone. If a line starts "We're the same, you and me..." cut it.
7. **Adults handing out wisdom.** Ikkei gets 1 coaching fact ("Your feet wait for it.") and 1 order to his grandson. Nekomata gets 1 compliment. Ukai never ends on a slogan (ch 21 review lesson).
8. **Mio cute overload.** She's a negotiator, not a mascot. Her biggest emotional moments are objects: puddings untouched, the empty doorway, the magnet.
9. **Therapy words.** Ban in dialogue and narration: trust, vulnerable, it's okay to rest, proud of you, healing, boundaries, safe.
10. **The release as a triumph.** It must cost a point and the match. Karasuno loses. If the release wins the match, the lesson curdles into "being good gets rewarded".
11. **Power creep through the soul door.** The eye fires 4 times in the arc, max. The release is not a new rank; it's a choice. The map's growth is shown rally by rally (Pace rule), not declared.
12. **POVs replaying.** Kenma's chapter ends at the hand; Sho's next chapter starts on the walk off. Akaashi's chapter covers the night; Sho's next starts at 6:30 a.m. with Ukai. No scene told twice.
13. **The secret leaking by accident.** Only Mio gets the eye's description, and in a child's words. Ikkei gets "where they're going". Nobody gets the name, ever (the bible: Sho never learns it).

## 7. One-paragraph pitch of the arc (for the bible)

Arc 3 is about a hand. Satoshi's stayed down in Osaka, and it cost him his knee. Ukai has asked Sho to put his up. In Tokyo, at set point against the team Karasuno has never beaten, it goes up, and the set goes with it. Hinata can't understand it, Kenma calls it logging out, and Sho buys his sister 5 puddings rather than tell her what the eye is like, and she won't eat them. An old coach tells him his feet are waiting for a miracle. A setter in Satoshi's old number tosses him the ball from his memory, and he spends the eye on purpose in an empty gym and stops himself with nobody watching. He puts his hand up 2 rallies late, confesses, and spends a day on the bench, where a kind old man offers him the dark room with the monitors and he says no thank you. On the last day he plays all 3 sets against Nekoma, the eye comes, he takes 1 read and lets it go, and they lose, and he laughs on the floor doing the penalty dive. At the barbecue he tells Hinata a name nobody knows. At home he tells Mio all of it, and she calls it fast-forward, eats 1 pudding, and moves the magnet to January. The new notebook says *Play all 5 sets.* He has only proved it where nothing counted.
