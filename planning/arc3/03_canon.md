## 0. Corrections and flags for the bible (read first)

1. **The 1st camp was probably not in Saitama.** Kenma says at the week-long camp that the Fukurodani Group rotates hosts and "the last one was at Fukurodani Academy" [web: snippet from the Stillness and Motion / Shinzen pages]. The anime adds that the week-long summer camp is always at Shinzen [web]. So: **trip 1 (the weekend "Tokyo away games", ch 78 to 81) = Fukurodani Academy, Tokyo. Trip 2 (the week-long summer camp, ch 85 to 96) = Shinzen High, Saitama.** The bible's "Tokyo (Saitama) camp" for ch 78 and the BRIEF's "Tokyo/Saitama" are mixing the 2. [unsure on trip 1 only because no snippet names the gym directly]
2. **"Rolling Thunder" is not an Arc 3 move.** Noya names it in Arc 1 (ch ~16/17, his intro) [web]; "Rolling Thunder Again" comes in Spring High prelims [memory]. In Arc 3 Noya's thread is learning to **set** (the libero jump toss from behind the 3 m line) [memory].
3. **Who names the top 3 aces is unsure.** The anime episode summary for the barbecue says **Bokuto** talks about rivals from all over the country [web]. My memory: Bokuto and Kuroo banter; the 3 named are **Ushijima Wakatoshi (Shiratorizawa), Kiryu Wakatsu (Mujinazaka, Oita), Sakusa Kiyoomi (Itachiyama, Tokyo)**, and Bokuto is "top 5, not top 3", which he hates [memory]. Sakusa's 1st mention is ch 97 [web, blog snippet]. The bible says Kuroo names them. Safest: Kuroo needles, Bokuto answers.
4. **"Fukurodani 25-23"** for the final camp match is in the bible's timeline but no source confirmed it. The wiki's Karasuno page lists Karasuno vs Fukurodani practice sets like **12-25 and 16-25** without saying which day [web]. The final match was Karasuno trying to "win at least 1 set" and **Fukurodani won** [web]. Use 25-23 only as a fic choice.
5. **Arc boundary.** Wikipedia/fandom place the Tokyo Expedition arc at **ch 72 to 96, anime S2 ep 1 to 11** [web]. Ch 97 ("Above") and 98 ("Conversations") bridge into Spring High prelims; anime S2 ep 12 "Let the Games Begin!" is already Spring prelims day 1 [web].
6. **Saeko's 1st appearance** is earlier than the drive: Tanaka and Noya study at Tanaka's house during exam prep (S2 ep 1) [web: "Nishinoya and Tanaka study at the latter's place"; Saeko there is memory].

## 2. Chronological beat sheet

### A. The invite and the exams (ch 72 to 73, 77; S2 ep 1)

- **The invite.** After the Inter-High loss to Seijoh, Takeda brings news: Nekoma (coach Nekomata) has invited Karasuno into the **Fukurodani Academy Group** training camp with **Fukurodani, Shinzen, Ubugawa** (and Nekoma) [web]. Ukai Keishin is now the formal coach [memory].
- **The catch.** Final exams. Anyone with a failing mark ("akaten", red mark) has make-up tests that clash with the trip, and the vice principal won't move them [web: "plead their desperate case to the vice principal to no avail"]. The danger list: **Hinata, Kageyama, Tanaka, Nishinoya** [web: "Hinata, Kageyama and two other players"; names memory].
- **Tutoring.** Tsukishima reluctantly tutors Hinata and Kageyama (Yamaguchi along) [memory, consistent with bible]. Tanaka and Noya study at Tanaka's house [web], where **Saeko** first shows up [memory]. Ennoshita helps the 2nd years [memory, unsure].
- **The result (ch 77 region).** Tanaka and Noya scrape through [memory]. **Hinata and Kageyama each fail** (at least 1 subject; memory says English for both, and a joke that one was a near miss) [web for the fail; subject memory/unsure]. They must sit the make-up test on the morning the team leaves.
- **Open detail:** exact marks and subjects unknown. Space for Sho (see section 5).

### B. Yachi's recruitment (ch 73 to 76; S2 ep 1 to 3)

- **Why:** Kiyoko is a 3rd year and needs a successor manager [memory]. She goes looking in the 1st-year halls [web: "Kiyoko searching the first-year hall"].
- **Who:** **Yachi Hitoka, 1st year, class 1-5** (MAL lists "first-year, Class 5", position "manager (trainee)", recruited by Shimizu) [web]. That matches the fic: Sho's class.
- **How:** Kiyoko asks her; Yachi says yes mostly because she can't refuse a beautiful senpai, then panics (her imagination runs to being sold off, etc.) [memory]. Yachi is a strong student and helps tutor Hinata and Kageyama [memory].
- **Ch 74 "Direct Sunlight":** Yachi sees the freak quick; the team is blinding to her [memory, title web].
- **Ch 75 "Villager B":** Yachi sees herself as "Villager B" (a background extra). She is drawn to the team but can't tell her mother [web]. Her mother (Yachi Madoka, an advertising designer, strict and busy) [memory]. **Hinata finds out why and acts at once** [web]; by the end she belongs and Hinata calls her "the best Villager B" [web, ANN review snippet]. Memory: Hinata's pitch is that even Villager B can choose to stand up and go fight.
- **Ch 76 "Creator":** Yachi designs a **fund-raising poster/flyer** so Karasuno can afford the Tokyo trips, using her design skills [memory; the "Fly" crow poster detail is unconfirmed by snippets]. She tells her mother, who accepts it as long as Hitoka chose it herself [memory]. She joins officially.
- **Note for the fic:** Sho must not do the recruiting or the "Villager B" speech. Hinata owns the push; Kiyoko owns the ask.

### C. Make-up test and Saeko's drive (ch 78; S2 ep 4)

- The team leaves early for the first trip. Hinata and Kageyama sit the make-up test [web].
- **Tanaka arranges his sister:** **Saeko drives them** to the camp [web: "Saeko Tanaka takes them to join Karasuno"; ANN ep 4 review]. Her driving is wild [web]. Tanaka "can't guarantee their safety in her car" [web, fan snippet].
- **In the car:** Saeko went to Karasuno and tells Hinata about the **Small Giant** era; she still remembers his face and "almost fell for him" [web]. Hinata talks about seeing Karasuno at nationals on TV [web].
- They arrive mid-day/afternoon on day 1. The team has already been losing [web: "Karasuno suffers many losses in their absence ... nine consecutive penalty laps of diving drills" (one source, may refer to this half-day)].

### D. Trip 1: the weekend "Tokyo away games" (ch 78 to 81; S2 ep 4 to 5)

- **Where / how long:** Fukurodani Academy, Tokyo (see flag 1). At least 2 days ("the second day of the Tokyo away games", ep 5) [web]. Likely a Sat-Sun weekend right after exams, early-mid July [memory/unsure].
- **Schools:** Nekoma, Fukurodani, Shinzen, Ubugawa, Karasuno [web].
- **Format:** round-robin practice games through the day; **every loss = a penalty**. Trip-1 penalty is the **"flying fall" lap**: a full diving lap of the court (dive, slide on the chest, get up, dive again) [web: "flying lap" penalty; "penalty laps of diving drills"]. Games are mostly **single sets to 25** [memory; consistent with the "win at least 1 set" goal and the 12-25 / 16-25 lines].
- **Ch 79 "Center Ace":** Hinata and Kageyama arrive and see **Bokuto** (Fukurodani #4, captain, ace) [web]. Hinata notices his quick is **less effective against faster, better opponents** [web]. A Shinzen player gets touches on the quick [web, snippet names Eikichi; memory says Chigaya Eikichi is a Shinzen 1st year who later appears at the youth camp].
- **Ch 80 "Desire/Greed":** Day 2. **Karasuno can't take a single set from Nekoma, especially with their new player, Lev** [web]. **Lev Haiba** (1st year MB, 194 cm, half-Russian, a volleyball beginner of a few months) uses his reach and reaction to **block / touch Hinata's quick** [web for "Lev promises he'll be the first to block it"; memory for the camp blocks]. Yaku hammers Lev on receiving [memory]. Kenma watches Hinata [memory].
- **Hinata's turn (ch 80 to 81):** Hinata realizes the quick depends on Kageyama alone; with eyes shut he cannot see the block. He wants to **open his eyes and fight in the air himself** [web: "he won't close his eyes for the freak quick anymore"].
- **Ch 81 "Room to Grow":** Kageyama refuses; their attempt sets the team back in practice games [web]. The weekend ends in losses. Karasuno heads home. [Exact record not found; memory: Karasuno won very little, maybe nothing, on trip 1.]

## 3. Arc 3 newcomers: voice and soul

Keep the fic's rule: short, plain, real. Below is what each person *wants* and how they *sound*.

### Kuroo Tetsuro (Nekoma #1, 3rd yr, MB, captain)
- **Voice:** lazy drawl, a smirk you can hear, teasing nicknames ("Tsukki"), half-questions that are traps ("Oh? You sure about that?"). Provokes on purpose. Switches to clear, technical coach-talk when he's teaching blocking; then right back to the grin.
- **Soul:** Kenma's oldest friend; brought him into volleyball and still feels responsible for it being fun. A natural teacher who likes other teams getting stronger (he wants the Garbage Dump battle at its best). Read blocker: patience, systems, making the spiker hit where *he* chose.
- **Fic use:** Kuroo is the one most likely to notice Sho's habits *out loud* and poke at them, and to teach Sho that the block can do part of the reading for the defense.

### Kozume Kenma (Nekoma #5, 2nd yr, setter)
- **Voice:** quiet, flat, few words, game vocabulary (level up, boss, grinding, save point). Calls Hinata "Shoyo". Doesn't like being looked at. Hates effort for its own sake.
- **Soul:** watches everyone to avoid surprises; plays because of Kuroo and because Hinata is "interesting". Doesn't care about camp wins; cares about puzzles. The brain of Nekoma.
- **Fic use:** already read Sho in Arc 2 (bible ch 14/15). He's the mirror: the other kid who reads the court. Keep him from becoming Sho's rival in intensity; he's a curious, tired observer.

### Haiba Lev (Nekoma #11, 1st yr, MB, 194 cm)
- **Voice:** loud, cheerful, confident to the point of comedy ("I'm going to be Nekoma's ace!"); takes scolding lightly; honest; says what he thinks.
- **Soul:** wants to be the star now, hates basics, is genuinely athletic and fast-learning. Half-Russian, a beginner in his first months. Rivalry with Hinata (height vs the little giant dream). Learns humility through receiving.
- **Fic use:** Sho's natural 1st-year foil: Lev's raw body vs Sho's reading. Both get bullied into receive drills by Yaku. Lev's block on Hinata's quick is canon: Sho mustn't take it.

### Yaku Morisuke (Nekoma #3, 3rd yr, libero, 165 cm)
- **Voice:** sharp, strict, a quick kick for Lev; a proper senpai. Dry humor. Furious at height jokes.
- **Soul:** pride in reception, the guardian of Nekoma's floor. Kind under the bark; teaches by making you repeat.
- **Fic use:** bible already plans libero training with Yaku. Yaku is the perfect teacher for "build the map so I don't need the eye": he reads by footwork and the hitter's shoulder, no magic.

### Bokuto Kotaro (Fukurodani #4, 3rd yr, WS, captain, ace)
- **Voice:** "Hey hey hey!", huge volume, all-caps joy, simple words, calls himself the best, praises loudly, sulks loudly. Asks for "one more!" spike forever.
- **Soul:** pure love of the game and of the *moment*. Moods swing on tiny things: a straight shut down, a cross blocked, being ignored. **Dejected mode** ("emo mode", "shobon" in fandom): head down, wants no sets, mumbles. Comes back fast on praise or a perfect set. Generous teacher (Hinata's eventual master).
- **Fic use:** his answer to Tsukishima ("the moment") is Tsukishima's, not Sho's. But Satoshi has *had* that moment and lost it; Bokuto is a mirror of the ace Satoshi was.

### Akaashi Keiji (Fukurodani #5, 2nd yr, setter, vice captain)
- **Voice:** calm, polite, deadpan, "Bokuto-san". Explains Bokuto to outsiders without embarrassment ("He's in his dejected mode. It happens.").
- **Soul:** the manager of a star. Watches tells and moods, adjusts the offense (stops setting Bokuto when he sulks, lets the others win points, then gives him the one ball that brings him back). Quietly worried about being "ordinary" (shown at Nationals).
- **Fic use:** Akaashi is the closest thing to an analyst on the court; a natural quiet friendship for Sho (both read people; neither says so). Do not let Sho "solve" Bokuto for Akaashi.

### Ukai Ikkei (Karasuno's old coach, Keishin's grandfather)
- **Voice:** gruff old man, blunt, laughs at the kids, teaches in plain physical terms ("whoever reaches the top first wins"). Warm under it.
- **Soul:** built Karasuno's glory years and the Small Giant; was hospitalized, now teaches children because it's fun. Sees through people fast.
- **Fic use:** bible: "1st adult who suspects the eye". The tempo lessons are Hinata's; Ikkei can watch Sho for 1 minute and ask what he's looking at.

### Yachi Hitoka (Karasuno manager, 1st yr, class 1-5)
- **Voice:** fast nervous patter, worst-case daydreams spoken aloud, over-polite, apologizes; gets brave in a sudden burst.
- **Soul:** sees herself as Villager B, a background character; wants to be useful; good student and visual designer like her mother. Chooses courage for herself.
- **Fic use:** Sho's classmate. Small kindness only, never her rescuer.

### Tanaka Saeko (Tanaka's older sister)
- **Voice:** loud, cheerful, tomboyish, calls her brother "Ryu", big laugh, drives like a maniac, plays taiko in a drum group (cheers with drums later).
- **Soul:** proud ex-Karasuno student who saw the Small Giant era; big-sister energy for the whole team.
- **Fic use:** the car ride is Hinata and Kageyama's; Sho isn't in that car (he's on the team bus).

### Shinzen High (Saitama) and Ubugawa High (Kanagawa)
- **Shinzen:** host of the week-long camp. Captain **Ogano Daiki** (3rd yr WS, curly "broccoli" hair) [web]. 1st year **Chigaya Eikichi** [web/memory]. Coach **Sasagawa Yoshiki** [bible]. They start reading Hinata's quick [web].
- **Ubugawa:** jump-serve school, **100 serves each after every practice** [web]. Captain **Goura Masaki** (3rd yr WS) [web]. Coach **Nakazaki Junichi** [bible]. Ogano and Goura are bitter rivals trading insulting nicknames [web].

### Fukurodani roster (others, for crowd scenes) [memory]

## 5. SPACES where Sho can act without stealing canon moments

Each space: why it's free, and an eyes-as-weapon idea that fits the bible's rules (map first, eye rare, hand up at the burning).

1. **Exam week (ch 72 to 73).** Canon covers 4 failing boys and Tsukishima's tutoring. Free: Sho's own exams (29-year-old brain), and helping the **2nd-year study group (Tanaka/Noya at Tanaka's house)**, where Saeko first appears. He can be the one who makes Tanaka pass by teaching him to "read the test" like film (question patterns). Doesn't touch Tsukki's tutoring of H&K. Also a chance to set up Saeko-Sho before the drive.
2. **Class 1-5 days (ch 73 to 76).** Yachi panics in class. Sho, who notices everything, can see her nerves (Meta Vision on people, not balls) and simply not make it worse. 1 kind act (lends her notes, or tells her "they're loud but they're not scary"). Must be after Kiyoko's ask, not before.
3. **Trip 1, the morning without Hinata and Kageyama (ch 78).** The biggest open space. Canon says only that Karasuno lost repeatedly and did many flying-fall laps while the pair was at the make-up test. Nobody records those games. **Suga sets, no freak quick, a thinner offense: Sho gets real minutes** against Tokyo teams. Ideal place for a **scanning-overload** episode: too many new players, 4 unfamiliar teams, penalties after every loss, eyes tired before noon. Lose everything; learn the most.
4. **Shinzen and Ubugawa games on both trips.** Mostly off-page in canon; scores unknown. Ubugawa's jump-serve barrage = Sho's **reception lab** (the D Reception stat); reading the server's toss and shoulder by map, and by **sound** (his new ear-reading). Shinzen starts reading Hinata's quick: Sho can *see how* they read it (they're keying on Kageyama's hands / Hinata's run) and tell **Ukai, not Hinata**, in his notebook. Feeds Ukai's "no new quick, 1st tempo only" call without making it Sho's.
5. **Nekoma day 2 (ch 80 to 81).** Lev's blocks on Hinata stay canon. Free: **Yaku vs Sho** in back-row reading, Kenma reading Sho again, **Lev and Sho** as 1st-year opposites. Karasuno takes 0 sets [web]; Sho can still win a private duel (e.g., stop reacting to Kenma's eyes, read Kenma's feet).
6. **The 3 weeks between camps (ch 82 to 84).** Canon spends them on the fight, Ikkei, Oikawa. Free:
   - Sho's **own training plan**: film from camp (Satoshi's analyst life), building the **field map** on paper first.
   - **Receiver for Asahi's jump serve and Yamaguchi's float** (he gets better, they get reps; their moments stay theirs).
   - A **separate Ikkei visit** (bible: Ikkei asks what Sho keeps looking at). Different day from Hinata's. Ikkei can give Sho something that isn't tempo: e.g., "watch the hips, not the eyes" or a blunt "you look like a man counting money."
   - Mio/Mom/doctor follow-ups; light practice rule if the eye fired at trip 1.
7. **Shinzen camp nights: the 3rd gym (ch 85 to 91).** Canon: Bokuto spikes, Akaashi sets, Kuroo and Tsukishima (and Lev) block, Hinata joins later. **Nobody plays defense behind that block.** Sho can be **the digger in the back** of the 3rd gym: the block-and-defense system Kuroo teaches only works with a defender reading off the block. Kuroo teaches Tsukishima where to put the block; **Sho learns where the ball goes when the block does its job.** That's a whole Meta Vision rung (field map = block + defense as one picture) earned honestly, and it never touches Tsukki's question or Bokuto's "moment" answer (Sho is silent in that scene, maybe off fetching balls).
8. **The 3v3 (ch 91).** Lineups unsure in sources. Either Sho is the extra (ref, ball boy, 7th man) or a separate night's 3v3 with him on Kuroo's side. Map only, no eye.
9. **Bokuto's moods.** Sho will clock the triggers in 1 game (analyst eyes). The space: he **notices and says nothing**, then watches Akaashi do it better than he could. A conversation with Akaashi (2 readers, low words) is free territory.
10. **Hill dashes and flying laps.** Pure fic texture. Good for the cost theme: physical exhaustion + eye fatigue, the hand-up rule tested when he's tired and losing.
11. **The fic's "Hand up" chapter (bible ch 38).** Any mid-camp game vs Shinzen/Ubugawa (low canon detail) is the right stage; never the final Fukurodani game.
12. **Final match vs Fukurodani (ch 94 to 96).** Canon details beyond the new quick and the result are thin. Sho can **play the whole set on the map** (bible ch 40 to 41), maybe read Fukurodani's non-Bokuto options (Konoha, Washio) since Bokuto is everyone's focus. Result stays a Fukurodani win; the new quick still lands then fails on its own.
13. **The barbecue (ch 97).** Free: Sho and Akaashi or Kenma; Kuroo teasing Sho; Bokuto asking Sho something dumb and big. When the top 3 aces are named, **Sho's reaction is Satoshi's**: he has been the ace, and he watched aces on tape for 9 years. Careful with meta (he doesn't know Haikyuu canon); the names can mean nothing to him, or the *type* of player can.
