## 3. Format and bracket

- **2 stages** [web: format, vol13]:
  1. **The 1st preliminary ("group" brackets).** Kakugawa was entered as "team 23, Group 4" [web: kakuhigh]. Karasuno vs Kakugawa was "the final match for Group 4"; the winner advances [web: kaku]. So each group is a small knockout bracket, and only its winner goes on.
  2. **The representative round, in October** [web: vol13]. One fan novel claims 16 teams: the Inter-High top 8 plus the 8 group winners [web: format, weak]. Karasuno were "seeded into the main bracket as team 10" [web: format, weak]. Bracket order in canon: **round 1 Johzenji, quarterfinal Wakutani Minami, semifinal Aoba Johsai, final Shiratorizawa** [web: johz, waku, seijoh, shira].
- **Bye.** Karasuno's 1st match (Ohgiminami) was **round 2** of the 1st preliminary [web: ohgi]. The reason is [unsure] (see 0.10).
- **Seeds straight into October** [memory]: Shiratorizawa, Seijoh, Date Tech and other Inter-High top teams don't play the 1st preliminary. Date Tech's Spring roster (Koganegawa as setter) plays in the October round. Seijoh beat Date Tech to reach the semifinal [web: vols, "Aoba Johsai vs Date Tech" in a volume listing; memory for the result].
- **Set counts** [memory, standard Japanese HS rules]: best of 3 (25, 25, 25) in every round **except the final**, which is best of 5 with a 15 point 5th set. Every set needs a 2 point lead. The web confirms that the 5th set "is played to 15" [web: s3sub] and that the final went 5 sets [web: shira].
- **Days.** The 1st preliminary was 1 day for Karasuno (Ohgiminami, then Kakugawa) [memory; the S2 ep 12 to 13 run fits]. The October round, by my memory: day 1 Johzenji and Wakutani, day 2 the Seijoh semifinal and then the final [memory, unsure]. Exact dates aren't in any snippet. I remember the final on **a Sunday in late October** [memory, unsure].
- **Venue.** Sendai City Gymnasium [memory]. The fic already says "the autumn in Sendai" (ch 45).

## 7. Questions the author must decide

1. **Which matches does Sho change, and how much?** The bible's hard results (beat Seijoh, then Shiratorizawa) stay. Do the smaller results (Ohgiminami 25-16, 25-13; Kakugawa 2-0; Johzenji 2-0; Wakutani 2-1) move? Changing a score in a lopsided match costs nothing; changing a 3 set win into a 2-0 changes Ennoshita's stint.
2. **Who replaces Kageyama against Johzenji in the fic?** If canon is Suga, that scene belongs to Suga. If the fic wants a Sho beat, it can't be setting (his Set is D).
3. **Daichi out against Wakutani.** Does Sho come on too (for Daichi's back row receive), or only Ennoshita? If both, Ennoshita has to keep the leadership beat and his late saves.
4. **Sho's 1st full 3 set match "that counts."** Seijoh (Oikawa's line from ch 25) or the final (5 sets)? Both can't be the 1st.
5. **Tsukishima's injury in set 3.** Canon puts a middle on court for set 4 and Hinata alone against Ushijima. Does Sho start set 4 in a reshuffle (wing), and how does he stay clear of "Hinata vs Ushijima"?
6. **Set 3 of the final: 18-25 or 20-25?** Pick 1 and mark it in the bible's timeline.
7. **The October gap.** Canon shows very little of it. How many chapters does the fic spend there on the ace program (weight room, reach, jump float, the knee)?
8. **"1 more" timing.** Set 4 of the final (29-27) is canon's high point, and Hinata owns its last ball. Sho's 1st "1 more" probably wants a different tight set: set 2's deuce (31-29) or late in set 3 against Seijoh.
9. **Tendou.** How much does Sho's eye "see" against a guess blocker? (By the author's rule it reads hitters and setters. Tendou is a blocker, so the eye shows Shirabu, and Tendou is the one thing it can't read: a good beat if he guesses Sho.)
10. **Does Ushijima notice Sho?** Canon's Ushijima notices only Hinata (and dismisses Oikawa's choice). A word to Sho after the final is a big beat. Decide if it's earned.
