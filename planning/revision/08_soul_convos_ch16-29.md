The bible's Arc 2 question is "will he stop watching and speak?" Read across 16 to 29, nearly every good conversation in the arc is about something a person won't say:

Design rule for the rebuild: **each chapter's 2 or 3 main conversations should each turn on one held-back thing.** Somebody almost says it, says the thing next to it, or says it plainly and lets it sit. Sho is shameless about asking for tosses and ruthless about hiding pain, and Arc 2 is where those 2 halves of him meet. That gives every scene a reason to exist without anyone delivering a speech.

**Retire on sight (from ch 16 and 17 especially):** the "retroactive" serve, "Define cute", "Read the contract" / "That's inflation" / "peer review" / "sample size" / "Consistency" (analyst and lawyer words out loud), Mio's polished one-liners ("Losing is quitting, except the other team does it for you"), the pancake exchange, the nikujaga detour, "Pets get fed... Most of these are mirrored toppers.

**Keep as models:** ch 18 (stairwell, read vs commit, Suga's confession), ch 21 (Kageyama on the bench), ch 23 (Mio), ch 25 (the sink and the clinic), ch 26's awning and Mom's kitchen, ch 27's lunch.
